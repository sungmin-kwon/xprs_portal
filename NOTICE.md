# Notices and attribution

XPRS Portal is an interface and literature-retrieval layer for exploring output
from eXplainable Polygenic Risk Score (XPRS) analyses.

The project incorporates XPRS methodology and legacy analysis material from:

> Kim N, Lee S. XPRS: a tool for interpretable and explainable polygenic risk
> score. *Bioinformatics*. 2025;41(4):btaf143.
> <https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btaf143>
