Portal task flow

Complete these activities first

Keep this page open in a separate tab while using the portal.

  1. Open the XPRS Information Portal and select the assigned individual ID.
  2. Generate an XPRS summary using the default top two contributing genes.
  3. Inspect the PRS density plot and individual Manhattan plot.
  4. Read the simple summary and at least one gene-level explanation.
  5. Open at least one cited reference and inspect the abstract viewer.
  6. Ask at least one follow-up question in the chatbot.
  7. Download or preview the full PDF report.
Suggested follow-up questions
  • What other cancers are the top genes related to?
  • Why is this gene important in this profile?
  • What does the PRS percentile mean?
  • How does XPRS calculate gene contribution scores?
