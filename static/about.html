XPRS Methodology

XPRS is an explainable polygenic risk score framework that decomposes individual genetic risk into gene-level and variant-level contributions.

article Read Bioinformatics Paper
Why XPRS

From a black-box PRS to the genes and variants behind it

Standard polygenic risk scores summarize many genetic effects into a single risk value. XPRS keeps that score but also shows which genes and variants contribute most, for a cohort or an individual.

Portal Layer

Explanations backed by the literature

For a chosen disease, the portal turns XPRS outputs into an interpretation layer: cohort risk plots, gene-level summaries with cited literature, and a follow-up Q&A.
