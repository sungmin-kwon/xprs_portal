COMMON + RARE VARIANT EVIDENCE

PRS and Rare-Variant Cohort Analysis

Do common- and rare-variant signals occur in the same individuals?

For the selected disease, this page compares PRS percentiles with rare-variant findings that the supplied K-PREDICT data explicitly assigned to that disease panel.

PRS percentile summarizes common-variant signal relative to applicable individuals in this cohort.

Panel alert is a supplied finding that may act with one copy and was designated for prominent review.

Carrier only is a supplied recessive heterozygous finding and is not classified as an alert.

The comparison is descriptive. It does not combine the two evidence types, establish causation, or provide a diagnosis.

How were disease-panel findings defined?

The supplied pipeline mapped pathogenic or likely pathogenic findings to diseases by gene membership in Genomics England PanelApp GREEN panels. PanelApp and ClinGen modes of inheritance were then used to classify each finding as an alert or carrier. The portal preserves those supplied assignments and does not infer them from ClinVar condition text.
